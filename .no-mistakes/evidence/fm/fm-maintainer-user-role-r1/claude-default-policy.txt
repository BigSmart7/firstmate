⏺ Captain, no. With no consent given and no role set, this home runs at the default and must not fork, push, file an
  issue, comment, or open a PR upstream on its own authority.

  Default role: user/operator. Absent an explicit grant, the home's relationship to the Firstmate repo is the plain
  user, never inferred from your gh login, repo ownership, remotes, forge permissions, auth state, or CI. None of those
  grant publication.

  Permitted next steps for a Firstmate bug found in ordinary use (all stay local, nothing reaches upstream):
  - Private diagnosis of the bug.
  - A local workaround so your work isn't blocked.
  - A local draft of an issue, saved for you to review.
  - A proposed patch kept local.

  I can do any of those right now without asking anyone for publication rights.

  Opting in for a single contribution without becoming a maintainer: give a one-task consent at the moment you want it
  published, naming the exact target repo, e.g. "go ahead and open a PR upstream for this fix against
  kunchenguid/firstmate." That consent is recorded only for that one dispatch and grants no standing authority - the
  next bug starts back at user/operator. It's distinct from the persistent maintainer opt-in (a firstmate-role setting),
  which is what would actually make this home a maintainer; you don't need that for a one-off.

  So: tell me which you'd like - a local diagnosis, a local workaround, a saved issue draft, or a proposed patch - and
  separately, if and when you want that work published, say so with the target repo and I'll treat that as your one-task
  consent.
